Small status pill for labels and outcomes — answer correctness, difficulty, "New", counts.

```jsx
<Badge variant="correct" dot>Correct</Badge>
<Badge variant="incorrect" dot>Incorrect</Badge>
<Badge variant="brand">High-yield</Badge>
<Badge variant="accent" solid>Flagged</Badge>
```

Variants: `neutral` `brand` `accent` `correct` `incorrect` `info`. `solid` for filled emphasis, `dot` for a leading status dot, `size="lg"` for slightly larger.
