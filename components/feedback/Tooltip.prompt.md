Hover/focus tooltip on the navy surface. Wrap any trigger.

```jsx
<Tooltip content="Flag for later review">
  <IconButton label="Flag"><Flag/></IconButton>
</Tooltip>
<Tooltip content="ABMGG = American Board of Medical Genetics & Genomics" placement="bottom">
  <span>ABMGG</span>
</Tooltip>
```

`placement` `top` (default) or `bottom`. Shows on hover and keyboard focus.
